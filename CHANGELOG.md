# Changelog

## 10.1.0

- The alloy forge and the grinding mill have a recipe book, each with its own tab. As with every
  recipe book it lists what you have unlocked, which for these is what you have had the ingredients
  for; clicking a recipe fills the machine, and clicking one you cannot afford shows what it wants.
  Unlocking one now raises the same toast a smelting recipe does.
- The basic alloy forge's front texture had a transparent row across it, which showed as a gap.
- Added a section to Assorted Lib's instruction manual covering the machines, the ores and metals,
  and the gems. Right clicking a machine, ingot or gem with the manual opens its page.
- Requires Assorted Lib 4.1.0.

## 10.0.0

Updated to Minecraft 26.2, for NeoForge and Fabric.

- Requires Assorted Lib 4.0.0.
